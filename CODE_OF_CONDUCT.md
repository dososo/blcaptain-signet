# Code Of Conduct

Signet is a design-system project. Keep discussion specific, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Critique artifacts, prompts, gates, and claims directly.
- Explain design objections with concrete evidence such as contact sheets, full-resolution samples, or failing gate output.
- Respect clean-room boundaries and contributor ownership.
- Help reviewers reproduce your results.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sustained personal attacks.
- Submitting copied third-party prompts, images, screenshots, names, or compositions.
- Pressuring maintainers to merge work that lacks gate evidence.
- Posting private information or credentials.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or reject contributions that violate these rules. Serious or repeated violations may result in a ban from project spaces.
