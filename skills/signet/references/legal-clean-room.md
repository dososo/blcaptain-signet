# Legal / Clean-Room Policy

Signet is an independent, clean-room project. It must never reproduce another product's
proprietary material. This file is the contributor-facing rulebook.

## Safe (do this)
- Study **publicly visible** pages and free/OSS resources for general understanding.
- Implement **original** style families with independent names/definitions (Group A–F).
- Use **standard, generic** design vocabulary (glassmorphism, claymorphism, cloisonné,
  risograph) — these are industry terms, not any company's property.
- Use **AI-generated example icons** produced by Signet's own prompts for the gallery.
- Ship generated icons for personal or commercial use (subject to the image model's own
  usage terms — see below).

## Risky / prohibited (never do this)
- Copy, re-type, paraphrase, extract, or repackage any commercial product's **Pro
  skills, prompt text, marketing copy, screenshots, icons, or brand assets**.
- Bypass any paywall or redistribute paid files.
- Reuse a third party's **named style set** verbatim, or clone its site copy/positioning.
- Use a product/company **name or logo lookalike**.
- Generate icons that imitate a **third party's trademarked logo**.

## Image-model usage terms (flag to users)
- Icons are generated by the host's image model (e.g. OpenAI gpt-image). **Output
  ownership and commercial-use rights follow that provider's terms**, not Signet's.
  Signet grants no rights over model output; it only orchestrates and exports.
- GPT Image models may require **API Organization Verification** before use.

## Recommended disclaimer (ship in README)
> Signet is an independent open-source tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
> derived from any commercial icon product, OpenAI, Apple, or Google. Icons are produced
> by third-party image models; their terms govern output ownership and use. Verify
> trademark and usage rights before shipping any generated icon.

## Contribution policy (clean-room)
- By contributing a style, you assert it is **your original work**, not copied from any
  commercial product's style set, and you license it under the repo's content license.
- New styles must have original names + definitions and pass the "no lookalike" review.
- Use **DCO sign-off** (`git commit -s`) — lightweight, no CLA needed for this Apache-2.0 repo.
